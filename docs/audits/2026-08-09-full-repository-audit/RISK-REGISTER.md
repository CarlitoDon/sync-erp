## Release gate

No production release should be called stable until at least `SEC-001`, `API-001`, `API-002`, `REL-001`, `QLT-001`, `GOV-001`, and the safety portion of `TST-001` have objective closure evidence. A merge commit, Vercel success, or green placeholder E2E job is not closure evidence.

## Severity interpretation

- **P0:** contain immediately; assume exposed credentials are compromised until rotated.
- **P1:** blocks a credible production release or creates a material security/reliability hazard.
- **P2:** scheduled engineering debt with measurable operational or quality cost.
- **P3:** hygiene/governance improvement with lower immediate risk.

Further aspect-specific findings are reconciled into this register only after their evidence is read and cross-checked; the master report is authoritative if wording differs.
